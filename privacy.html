Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 18, 2026  ·  Last Updated: February 18, 2026

SoundSwipe ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the SoundSwipe mobile application.

By using SoundSwipe, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the SoundSwipe service:

  • Spotify Account Data — When you connect your Spotify account, we access your display name, profile image, and email address provided by Spotify.
  • Playlist & Track Data — We access your Spotify playlists and song library solely to display them within the app and perform actions you initiate (keeping, removing, or moving songs).
  • Authentication Tokens — We store Spotify OAuth tokens locally on your device using secure storage to keep you logged in between sessions.
  • Usage Data — We may collect anonymous usage analytics such as feature interactions and crash reports to improve the app experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to operate the SoundSwipe app:

  • To authenticate you with your Spotify account
  • To display your playlists and tracks inside the app
  • To execute playlist actions you take (swipe keep, swipe remove, move songs)
  • To maintain your session so you do not need to log in on every visit
  • To diagnose bugs and improve app performance

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except as described below:

  • Spotify — SoundSwipe operates through the Spotify Web API. Any actions taken in SoundSwipe (adding or removing songs) are executed directly through Spotify's API on your behalf. Spotify's own Privacy Policy governs their data practices.
  • Apple — If you use iOS in-app purchases, Apple processes payment data under their own Privacy Policy. We do not receive your full payment information.
  • Analytics Providers — We may use anonymous crash reporting and analytics tools to understand app performance. No personally identifiable information is shared.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your Spotify authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage with platform-level security. We do not store your Spotify credentials or tokens on our servers.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain locally stored tokens only for as long as you remain logged into the app. When you log out, all authentication tokens and cached user data are deleted from your device immediately.

If you delete the SoundSwipe app, all locally stored data is removed from your device.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Disconnect Spotify — You can revoke SoundSwipe's access to your Spotify account at any time via your Spotify Account Settings under "Connected Apps."
  • Logout — Logging out of SoundSwipe clears all locally stored tokens and session data.
  • Delete the App — Uninstalling SoundSwipe removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Access & Correction — SoundSwipe displays data directly from Spotify. To update your profile information, do so through the Spotify app or website.

7. Children's Privacy

SoundSwipe is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you through the app.
